What MooseMode does

Thirteen modules, one settings window, hold Shift to skip any of it once.

◆Vendors

  • Sells every grey the moment a vendor opens
  • Repairs all gear, guild funds optional
  • Gold earned and spent reported in chat

◆Quests

  • Accepts, hands in, and picks up follow-ups from any quest window or gossip menu
  • Skips grey or green quests by your rule, and says why at the bottom of the window
  • Reward choices show their vendor value; the best one is framed in gold

◆Loot

  • Takes everything the instant loot is ready
  • Optionally leaves greys on the corpse

◆Combat

  • /petattack macros for every damage spell
  • Bar buttons kept on your highest spell rank

◆Interface

  • One combined bag, sorted on open, greys grouped
  • Macro names hidden, camera zoom unlocked
  • Beta Issue Reporter tucked away
/moose opens the settings dialog
Settings are account-wide and survive the beta client's saved-variable bug
